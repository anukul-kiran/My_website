How I decide an agent is ready

An agent gives a slightly different answer every time you ask, so one passing test doesn't tell you much. Every change goes through the same two checks before pilot users see it.

  1. A change to an agent New capability, prompt or data source
  3. Does it match the data? The answer agrees with what the underlying data says for the question asked. No invented figures. If not, it goes back
  5. Can a marketer act on it? Accurate isn't enough. It has to point to a next step a campaign team would take. If not, it goes back
  7. Pilot marketers use it And tell us where it still falls short
Their feedback becomes the next set of requirements

The suite works across Adobe Journey Optimizer, Adobe Experience Platform, Marketo Engage and Pega Customer Decision Hub.

Research

Dec 2024 to Aug 2025. Junior research fellow at SRM Institute of Science and Technology on a DRDO-funded programme for classifying objects in underwater sonar, with the Department of Ocean Engineering at IIT Madras.

Reading the shadow as well as the highlight

In side-scan sonar an object shows up twice: a bright return where sound bounces off it, and a dark acoustic shadow behind it where sound can't reach. The shadow often says more about an object's shape, but it's also the part noise destroys first. Our model reads both regions and learns, image by image, how much weight to give each.

Classification accuracy on real sonar images (OCEANS 2025)
Context-adaptive fusion (our model)96.75%
Shadow and highlight classifier92.18%
Shadow-only classifier85.98%

What we built along the way

  • A denoiser that keeps shadows intact. A U-Net with squeeze-and-excitation blocks and a Grad-CAM-guided loss. Best structural similarity (SSIM 0.64) of the seven methods we compared.
  • S3Simulator+. A benchmark of 7,000 to 8,000 simulated sonar images of ships, planes and naval mines, with physics-informed noise for multipath interference, backscatter and reverberation.
